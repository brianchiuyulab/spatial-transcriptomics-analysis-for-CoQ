# Spatial transcriptomics methods

## Source dataset

Spatial transcriptomic data were obtained from Walter *et al.* (*Nature Aging*, 2024; GSE266933). Female C57BL/6J mice were injured in the tibialis anterior muscle by intramuscular notexin injection, and transverse 10 µm sections were collected at 5 days post injury. The present analysis used the young 5-month and geriatric 26-month sections profiled with the Curio Seeker/Slide-seq platform.

Raw count matrices were obtained from GEO accessions GSM8257020 and GSM8257021. Spatial coordinates, injury-zone classifications, quality-control metrics, and cell2location outputs were obtained from the processed H5AD objects deposited by the authors in Dryad. GEO metadata identify the young sample as 5 months old; this value was retained here.

## Published preprocessing and annotation

According to the published methods, reads were processed with slidesnake using the `seeker_v3.1_internalTrim` workflow, Cutadapt 4.6, and STAR Solo 2.7.10b against GRCm39. Beads with fewer than 20 transcripts, genes detected in fewer than 10 beads, and beads with fewer than 10 neighboring beads within 100 µm were removed. The public analysis notebook normalizes each bead to 10,000 counts followed by `log1p` for Scanpy visualizations.

Cell-type abundance was estimated with cell2location v0.1.3 using a matched single-cell reference. Published reference-gene filters were `cell_count_cutoff = 5`, `cell_percent_cutoff = 0.01`, and `nonz_mean_cutoff = 1.12`; `detection_alpha = 20` was reported. The paper reports `N_cells_per_location = 1`, whereas the public example notebook sets `N_cells_per_location = 4`. This reanalysis does not refit cell2location and therefore does not depend on that discrepant setting. The processed objects contain q05-derived cell-type values normalized by their row sum; these 15 proportions sum to one for every retained bead. As in the public notebook, each bead was displayed using the cell type with the largest proportion. This is a dominant-label summary of a spatial bead, not a new cell annotation performed in this release.

The authors defined the initial injury region from the number of immune-cell labels among each bead's 300 nearest neighbors, using an immune-neighbor threshold greater than 20 together with sample-specific spatial boundaries. The region was refined with a 20-nearest-neighbor graph by requiring more than 10 injury-labeled neighbors. Deposited injury classifications were used without modification.

## Coq8a analysis

Raw Coq8a counts were aligned to the processed metadata by bead barcode. A bead was designated Coq8a-positive when its raw count was at least one. Quality-control summaries were recomputed from the raw matrices; mitochondrial genes were identified case-insensitively by the `mt-` prefix. Mitochondrial percentage was reported descriptively and was not used as an additional exclusion rule in the primary analysis.

Within each section's deposited injury zone, enrichment of each cell label among Coq8a-positive beads was evaluated by a two-sided Fisher exact test. P values were adjusted by the Benjamini-Hochberg procedure separately within each section across the 15 deposited cell labels. This family corresponds to the complete cell-label screen shown in the analysis table.

Cell-label associations are displayed as odds ratios with 95% confidence intervals. If any cell of the corresponding 2 × 2 table was zero, 0.5 was added to all four cells only to obtain a finite odds ratio and confidence interval for plotting (Haldane–Anscombe correction). Exact Fisher P values and their Benjamini–Hochberg adjustments were calculated from the uncorrected counts.

Six differentiation/fusion markers (*Myh3*, *Myh8*, *Myog*, *Mymk*, *Mymx*, and *Ncam1*) were summarized descriptively within Fusing Myocytes-labelled and other injury-zone beads. Dot size represents the percentage of beads with at least one detected transcript; color represents mean per-bead expression after total-count normalization to 10,000 and `log1p` transformation.

To test spatial proximity to fusion-associated regions, Coq8a-positive density was calculated within 25, 50, 75, 100, and 150 µm of beads labeled Fusing Myocytes. The null distribution was generated by 2,000 random selections of the same number of injury-zone focal beads. P values were adjusted across the 10 sample-by-radius tests. Robustness was examined at 50 µm after applying alternative raw-count or mitochondrial-percentage filters; these filters were not substituted for the published primary bead set.

As a complementary check, injury-zone beads were aggregated into square spatial blocks of 100, 150, 200, or 250 µm, retaining blocks with at least 10 beads. Spearman correlations related the local Fusing Myocytes fraction to Coq8a detection and mean expression; significance was assessed with 5,000 label permutations and Benjamini-Hochberg adjustment across eight tests per endpoint.

## Interpretation limits

The design contains one spatial section from one mouse per age. Age-related differences are descriptive and no population-level age inference is made. Curio Seeker beads can receive transcripts from more than one nearby cell; enrichment for a cell2location label does not establish cell-autonomous expression. No matched H&E image or segmented fiber morphology was deposited for these sections, so histologic fusion index, myotube number, and fiber or myotube width cannot be calculated from this dataset.
