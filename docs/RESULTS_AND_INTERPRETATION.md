# Results and interpretation

## Dataset and quality control

The young and geriatric raw matrices contained 77,642 and 78,514 beads, respectively. The deposited final datasets contained 32,104 young and 22,920 geriatric beads after the authors' transcript-count and spatial-neighbor filters. Median raw counts per retained bead were 53 and 47. Coq8a was detected in 465 young beads (1.45%) and 243 geriatric beads (1.06%). The sparse detection rate is expected for bead-level spatial data and makes direct MuSC-only analysis underpowered.

## Cell-type and fusion-associated evidence

Within the injury zone, 44 of 149 Coq8a-positive young beads were labeled Fusing Myocytes, compared with an 18.14% Fusing Myocytes baseline among all young injury beads (relative enrichment 1.63; Fisher p = 5.75 × 10⁻⁴; BH-adjusted p = 0.00862). In the geriatric section, 21 of 63 Coq8a-positive beads were labeled Fusing Myocytes, compared with a 14.31% baseline (relative enrichment 2.33; Fisher p = 1.65 × 10⁻⁴; BH-adjusted p = 0.00247).

The Fusing Myocytes regions also showed higher detection of *Myh3*, *Myh8*, *Myog*, *Mymk*, *Mymx*, and *Ncam1* than other injury-zone beads. This marker audit supports the biological interpretation of the deposited label as a differentiating/fusion-associated myogenic state.

## Spatial neighborhood evidence

In the young section, Coq8a-positive density around Fusing Myocytes foci exceeded random injury-zone foci at all tested radii from 25 to 150 µm after correction. In the geriatric section, the association was not resolved at 25 or 50 µm but was detected at 75–150 µm. The small-radius geriatric result changed with alternative quality filters, whereas the broader-radius association was more stable. These results support local spatial association, not ligand–receptor signaling or causal regulation.

The injury zone as a whole was not Coq8a-high: detection was 1.30% inside versus 1.53% outside in the young section, and 0.65% versus 1.36% in the geriatric section. Thus the signal is localized to particular injury-zone niches rather than reflecting uniform induction throughout injured tissue.

## What can be stated in the manuscript

Supported statement: **Coq8a expression is spatially associated with transcriptionally defined differentiating/fusing myogenic niches during muscle regeneration.**

Unsupported statements: Coq8a is definitively expressed by individual fusing myocytes; Coq8a causes fusion; aging changes Coq8a expression at the population level; or Coq8a alters fusion index, myotube number, or myotube width. Those claims require biological replicates, cell-resolved validation, perturbation, and matched histology or in-vitro fusion assays.
